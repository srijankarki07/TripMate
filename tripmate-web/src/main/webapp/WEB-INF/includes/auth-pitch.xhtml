TripMate

Plan the trip. Split the costs. Settle up.

One place for the plan and the money, for a small group of people who are actually travelling together.

  • A shared itinerary Day by day, with times and places, edited by everyone.
  • Expenses that split fairly Equally, or by exact shares, across any subset of the group.
  • Who owes whom, always current Balances settle themselves as expenses and repayments land.
  • Nobody has to be chased Invitations and a summary when the trip ends, by email.
  • Your calendar, kept in step Connect Google Calendar once and the plan appears in it and follows every change. Optional, and removable at any time.

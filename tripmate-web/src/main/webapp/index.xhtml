How it works

Four steps, and the last one is the point.

It takes about a minute to get a trip going. Everything after that is the group filling it in.

  1. 01

    Create the trip

    A name, the dates, and where it is going.

  2. 02

    Invite the group

    They join with their own account and see the same plan.

  3. 03

    Plan the days

    Add what happens and when. Anybody can edit it.

  4. 04

    Log it and settle up

    Whoever paid records it, and the balances do the rest.

Everest Base Camp, 5 to 20 November. Every item in the plan becomes an event in your own calendar, at the time the plan gives it.

The plan can appear in the calendar you already look at, and follow every change to it. Connecting it is optional: everything else works without it.

  • A change to the plan changes the event.
  • Deleting an item removes it again.
  • Times are written in the trip's own timezone.

Connecting it happens from inside a trip, and you can disconnect from the same place.

One item of the plan becomes one event, like this:

Fly to Lukla

Thu 5 November 2026, 06:00 to 07:00

Tribhuvan International Airport

Everest Base Camp Trek, kept in step by TripMate

The permission it asks for, what is stored and for how long, and how to take the access back are set out in full in the privacy policy.

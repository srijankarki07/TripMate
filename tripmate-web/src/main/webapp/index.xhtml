TripMate

A shared itinerary and a running record of who paid for what, for a small group of travellers. The business tier holds the rules, the persistence tier maps the seven tables, and the web tier only presents.

Where the project is

  • Phase 0: EAR deploys to GlassFish 7 as a Jakarta EE 10 application
  • Phase 1: seven linked tables in PostgreSQL, with sample data
  • Phase 2: seven JPA entities mapped and round-tripping
  • Phase 3: nine DTOs as the remote boundary contract
  • Phase 4: five session beans with remote interfaces, full CRUD

The sign-in flow and the trip pages arrive in Phase 6. Until then, exercises the business tier end to end.

Jakarta EE 10 · GlassFish 7 · PostgreSQL

You are responsible for what you add. In particular, do not put anybody else's personal information into a trip without their agreement.

5. Connecting Google Calendar

Connecting a calendar is optional, and you can disconnect at any time as described in the privacy policy. If you connect it, you are asking TripMate to write the itinerary of your trips into that calendar and keep it up to date. You can remove any event it writes, and doing so does not affect the trip.

The Google permission you grant is between you and Google. This application's handling of what it receives under that permission is governed by the privacy policy, and it makes no other use of it.

6. Shared trips and money

TripMate records what people say they spent and works out who owes whom. What it produces is arithmetic on what was entered, not financial advice, and it is not a payment service. No money moves through the application. Settling up happens between you, by whatever means you choose, and the application only records that it happened.

If a trip's figures matter, check them. Everyone on a trip can add and change entries, so a balance is only as good as what was typed in.

Who has spent what

What each person has put in, against what their share of the trip comes to. A positive difference means they are owed it back.

Who is up and who is down

These are the gross positions: every split counts, whether or not it has been repaid, and recorded repayments are not netted off. Recording a repayment changes what is still outstanding, not these figures.

Invite someone

Enter the full email address of someone who already has a TripMate account. There is no list of users to browse, on purpose: who else has an account here is not something a trip organiser needs to know.

On this trip

An administrator can invite and remove people and change the trip's details. A trip needs at least one, so the last one cannot be removed or step down.

Leave this trip

You stop seeing it, and it stops appearing in your balances. Your expenses stay on the trip.

Send everyone the summary

Emails each person on the trip what it cost, what their own share came to, and whether they are owed money back or still owe some. Useful once the trip is over and somebody asks.

Trip settings

Delete this trip

Removes the itinerary, every expense and split, and all recorded repayments. The people themselves are not deleted.

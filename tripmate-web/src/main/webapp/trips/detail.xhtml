Who has spent what

What each person has put in, against their share of the trip, with any repayments already recorded counted. A positive amount is money they should get back.

Who is up and who is down

Left to settle is what still has to move for everybody to be even: what each person paid, minus their share, plus what they have paid back, minus what they have received. A positive amount is money they should get back.

At a glance

Each member's position, measured from the centre line. Bars to the right are money they should get back; bars to the left are money they still have to pay. Every bar is drawn against the largest position on the trip, so the lengths are comparable with each other.
